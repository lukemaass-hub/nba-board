Add individual games

Columns: date (YYYY-MM-DD), time_et (24h), away, home, optional note.

Minutes (from your sheet)

Paste the sheet as CSV. Needs a player column and an average-minutes column; a header row with player, team, mpg is detected automatically.

Injuries

Columns: team, player, status (Out, Doubtful, Questionable, Probable), optional note, through date and source. Use it for beat-writer tips and ESPN items, e.g. NYK,Jose Alvarado,Questionable,Knee soreness,2026-10-12,Beat writer.

Depth charts

Fill in teams that are missing, or replace one. Columns: team, position (PG, SG, SF, PF, C), tier (S starter, R rotation, L limited), player, in depth order. A team in the paste is fully replaced.
